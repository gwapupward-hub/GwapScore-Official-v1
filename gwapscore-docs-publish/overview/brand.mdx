The GwapScore emblem is the **Scoreboard Crest**: a crowned scoreboard enclosure framing a custom segmented "GS", with side ticks and a three-step progression signature beneath the letters.

## Usage

### Do

- Use the full crest at normal display sizes.
- Use the GS Micro Mark when the full enclosure is too small to reproduce cleanly (roughly under 48px wide).
- Keep clear space around the emblem equal to at least one side-tick width.
- Place the emblem on black or very dark backgrounds.
- Use the `currentColor` SVG when the emblem must follow a UI theme.

### Don't

- Stretch, skew, or rotate the emblem.
- Rearrange or replace the custom segmented GS.
- Recolor the emblem outside approved colors or add effects, shadows, or gradients.
- Place the emblem on busy or low-contrast backgrounds.

## Brand Voice

GwapScore represents trust, precision, and clarity in reputation assessment. Our brand voice is:

- **Professional** — we take trust seriously.
- **Clear** — we communicate simply and directly.
- **Evidence-first** — we show the evidence behind every score.
- **Inclusive** — we build for everyone.
